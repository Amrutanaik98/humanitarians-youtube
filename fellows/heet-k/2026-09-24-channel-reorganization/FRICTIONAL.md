## 2026-09-27 — deciding what the channel is for

> Written 2026-09-28, one day after the week it describes. Reconstructed from the
> Week 17 report; not a contemporaneous entry.

- **Evidence:** NOT PROVIDED — see [README.md](README.md#evidence-gap--stated-plainly)
- **Hours:** 10 hrs reorganization + 8 hrs planning — [HOURS.md](../HOURS.md)

**What I was working on.** Stepping back from making individual videos to shaping
the container they go in: cleaning up what was already on the Medhavy channel, and
starting to work out what kinds of video should be added.

**What I tried, and what I expected.** I expected the cleanup to be the easy half
and the plan to be the hard half. The cleanup was easier, but not for the reason I
thought — it was easy once I stopped trying to decide the plan first. My initial
approach was to design the structure and then remove whatever didn't fit it. That
stalled, because I could not settle the structure in the abstract.

**Where it resisted, and what I did next.**

- **I could not plan the channel before seeing what it actually contained.**
  I reversed the order: unlist or remove what plainly did not belong, then look at
  what was left and ask what it implied. Cleaning first made the plan a reading of
  the remainder rather than an invention.
- **Unlist versus remove was a real decision each time.** Unlisting keeps a video
  reachable by link and preserves anything pointing at it; removing does not. I do
  not have a written rule for which I applied where, which means the decision is
  not reproducible.
- **The plan did not finish, and I labelled it Phase 1 rather than rounding up.**
  It is a first pass at what video types to add. Calling it a channel strategy
  would have been the easy write-up and the wrong one.
- **Nobody reviewed any of it.** Every other thread in this folder eventually went
  to the professor. This did not. Rather than imply a validation that didn't
  happen, the week is recorded as self-directed and unconfirmed.

**What I understand now, and what I still do not.**

I understand that organizing the container is different work from filling it, and
that it does not produce an artifact — which is exactly why it needs writing down.
A week that makes nothing can still change what gets made next.

What I do not have is any record. I did not capture the channel before or after,
did not keep a list of what was unlisted versus removed, and did not write the
Phase 1 plan down anywhere outside the weekly report. So the week's actual output
— a decision about the channel's shape — exists only as a claim. That is the thing
to fix, and it is more important than the plan itself: a plan nobody can read is
not a plan anyone can disagree with.

I also do not know whether the structure is right, because no one has seen it.
